Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we don't

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Short version: there are no accounts. Your game progress stays in your own browser. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which features people use, and we may show Google ads, which use cookies.

Who runs YouDumb

YouDumb (youdumb.netlify.app) is an independent project. For anything about this policy, email pratapneeraj240@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

You can play the first five levels of each arena without an account. Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. This includes:

  • Levels completed, stars, streak and daily results
  • Your playbook, mistakes list and lessons completed
  • The first name you type for challenge links, if you type one

You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The "save code" feature lets you copy this data yourself; we never see it.

Challenge links and sharing

When you copy a challenge link or share a result, the link contains your first name (if you entered one), your score and the level. It is only shared where you choose to post or send it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. It collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, the site that referred you, and game events like levels started and finished, scores, arena chosen and share button clicks. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this.

We do not collect your name, email address or any account details through analytics. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Advertising

YouDumb is free and is supported by ads served through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Accounts and the leaderboard

To play past the first era (ELIZA) you create a free account with Google or an email sign-in link. Accounts are run by Supabase, our database and login provider. We store:

  • Your email address (and, with Google sign-in, the basic profile Google shares: name and email)
  • The leaderboard name you choose
  • Your stars per level and arena, when you earned them, and your daily run scores and times

Your leaderboard name, stars and daily scores are shown publicly on the global leaderboard. Your email is never shown. We use your email only to sign you in and, if we ever send updates, you can unsubscribe at any time.

AI-written questions

Some questions are written on the fly by an AI model. To make them, our server sends the arena, the difficulty and the short text of questions you recently answered (so the AI doesn't repeat them) to OpenRouter, which passes it to the model provider. We don't send your name, scores or any other personal information, and the API key stays on our server.

Cookies

YouDumb itself does not set cookies; if you sign in, your login session is kept in your browser's local storage. Cookies on this site come from Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the game still works without them.

Children

YouDumb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete your account and leaderboard data at any time. Data held by Google can be managed through your Google account and the links above. If you have a question or request, email pratapneeraj240@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
