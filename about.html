About

AI got smarter every year. YouDumb checks if you did.

YouDumb is a free brain game for the age of AI. You climb a ladder of AI "eras", starting against ELIZA, a chatbot from 1966, and working up through GPT-2, GPT-3, ChatGPT and GPT-4 until you face today's frontier models. Every level is five quick questions. Beat the bot's score to move on.

Why it exists

It's easy to hand every question to a chatbot now. YouDumb is a daily reason to think for yourself first, and a way to see whether you're actually getting sharper or just getting better at prompting.

How it works

  • Nine arenas: Mixed, Logic & puzzles, Math, Aptitude, Words & language, Data & stats, Business & product, Street smarts and Coding. Each has its own 30-level ladder.
  • AI-made questions: fresh questions are written by AI and double-checked before you see them, so you never get the same question twice.
  • Fair timing: AI answers in seconds, so ties are judged against a human par time based on how much there is to read, any code to trace, and difficulty.
  • A trick with every question: each answer comes with a reusable rule, collected in your playbook. Questions you miss come back in "Fix my mistakes" until you get them right.
  • Two-minute lessons: each arena has short lessons on skills that keep you ahead of AI, with a drill at the end.
  • The frontier: the final era unlocks only after a perfect score on every level before it.

About the bots

The AI bosses are simulated. Each one answers with the rough accuracy and speed of its era; the game does not call those models live. Model names are used only to mark eras of AI. YouDumb is not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or any other AI lab.

Free, supported by ads

YouDumb is free with no sign-up. It's kept running by a small number of ads, which never appear while you're answering a question. Your progress stays on your device; see the privacy policy.
